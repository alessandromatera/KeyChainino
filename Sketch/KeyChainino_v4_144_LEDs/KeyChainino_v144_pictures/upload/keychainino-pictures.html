How it works

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (WebUSB). Plug a USBasp into USB.
  2. Connect the ISP cable to the KeyChainino's 2×3 pads (pin 1 is the square pad).
  3. Drawing: tap a light, or press and drag. Easier for small hands: hold the space bar and just move the mouse over the lights. The first light touched decides: off → the stroke switches lights on, on → it switches them off.
  4. The KeyChainino shows your child's drawings and the pictures you chose (at first: smile, heart, star, dog, car). Hold ✏️ on the shelf to add or take off pictures and drawings, then Save.
  5. Your child draws (or picks a picture), then presses 🚀. A picture picked and left unchanged goes on with its animation; anything drawn or changed goes on as a drawing. The first time, the page installs the Pictures program (about 15 s). This replaces any games on the KeyChainino.
  6. Every send after that only writes the drawing (about a second). The KeyChainino restarts and shows it straight away.
  7. On the KeyChainino: A / B flip through the pictures, A+B together makes the picture move. It sleeps after 30 s; press A+B to wake it.

Up to 40 drawings fit. When it is full, sending a new one removes the oldest.
