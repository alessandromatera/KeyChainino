How exact is this?
Emulated at instruction level (avr8js): every AVR instruction and cycle at 8 MHz — Timer0 (millis), Timer1 (game tick), Timer2 (display refresh), INT0/INT1, pin-change logic, EEPROM (persisted in this browser), and ADC noise for randomSeed().
  • The display is not read from the firmware's buffer: the page watches the 13 charlieplex pins (D4–D10, A0–A5) and lights an LED only while one pin sources HIGH and another sinks LOW — brightness is the exact fraction of CPU cycles each LED spends conducting. Ghosting, scan artifacts and dimming all reproduce.
  • Slow the speed to 1/1000× to watch the Timer2 row scan sweep the matrix.
  • Approximations: power-down sleep executes as a spin (wake gesture and display behave identically; only µA-level power states are meaningless here), the power reduction register is ignored, and brown-out/watchdog fuses are not modeled.
